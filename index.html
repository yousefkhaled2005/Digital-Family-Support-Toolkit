Emergency Instructions

  • Ensure Safety: Physical safety is the priority. Remove any harmful objects.
  • Stay Calm: Speak in a clear, soft, and non-threatening voice.
  • Do Not Argue: Avoid arguing with hallucinations or delusions.
  • Seek Help: Call for professional help immediately if the situation escalates.
